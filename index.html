My musical journey

Tripti Sharma is a promising Indian classical and semi-classical vocalist, deeply rooted in the rich traditions of the Banaras Gharana. Her repertoire includes Khayal, Thumri, Tappa, Dadra, Kajri, Holi, Jhoola, Chaiti, and Bhajan, with themes of spirituality, peace, love, and devotion.

Hailing from the brass city of Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh, Tripti began her journey at a young age under Pandit Brij Gopal Vyas Ji, who gave her the foundation in Indian classical music and bhajans.

She moved to Delhi and earned a Doctor of Philosophy in Indian Classical Music from Delhi University, with doctoral research on the contributions of Bharat Ratna Pandit Bhimsen Joshi Ji. She also holds the Sangeet Prabhakar from Prayagraj in Music Vocal, Instrument (Tabla), and Kathak Dance.

She went on to study with her guru Ms. Sunanda Sharma Ji, an illustrious representative of the Banaras Gharana. In the guru-shishya parampara she lived with her guru and accompanied her to performances across India and abroad.

Tripti is a Guest Assistant Professor at Kalindi College, Delhi University and a graded artist on All India Radio (AIR). Based in Stockholm, Sweden, she promotes Indian music through performances, cross-cultural collaborations, and workshops.
